About Us
Knockel's Floor Covering is a trusted flooring store located in Monona, IA, and has been proudly serving the surrounding areas since 1980. Specializing in premium flooring solutions, we offer a wide range of options including carpet, tile, vinyl, wood, porcelain, laminate, and stone. Our dedicated team provides expert installation services along with personalized remodel and design consultations for bathrooms, kitchens, bedrooms, and beyond. At Knockel's Floor Covering, we are committed to helping you transform your space with quality materials and exceptional craftsmanship.
