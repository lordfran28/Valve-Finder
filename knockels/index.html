On Center Street since 1980

Knockel's has sold floors and put them down in Monona for over forty years. Carpet, tile, vinyl, wood, porcelain, laminate and stone.

Most jobs start the same way. Somebody comes in with a picture on their phone, or a cabinet door under one arm, and says the kitchen floor has had it. We walk the samples together. We ask about the dog and the kids and the mud that comes in every March. Then we find a floor that will look right in that room and hold up for a long time.

Bathrooms, kitchens, bedrooms, the whole house. We measure, we order, and our own crew does the installing.

Come by and see the samples in person. Bring the picture.

Pick from our floors and see them in a picture of your own room, in the light you actually have.
